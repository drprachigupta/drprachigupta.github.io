About

My research focuses on understanding tumor–immune interactions, mechanisms of immune suppression, therapeutic target validation, and the development of preclinical models to advance cancer research.


My research experience spans ovarian cancer, breast cancer, renal cell carcinoma, melanoma, tumor microenvironment, extracellular vesicles, T-cell biology, immunotherapy, biomarker discovery, and translational cancer research.

Research Interests

Immuno-Oncology

Understanding mechanisms governing immune responses against tumors and identifying approaches to improve cancer immunotherapy.

Tumor Microenvironment

Investigating tumor–immune interactions and molecular mechanisms that contribute to immune suppression.

T-Cell Biology & TILs

T-cell functional assays, tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes, tumor-reactive T-cell generation and cytotoxicity studies.

Extracellular Vesicles

Studying tumor-derived extracellular vesicles and their role in immune regulation and cancer progression.

3D Tumor & Organoid Models

Development and application of preclinical models including clear cell renal cell carcinoma organoid systems.

Translational Cancer Research

Connecting mechanistic cancer biology with preclinical therapeutic target validation and clinically relevant research questions.

Research Experience

Earle A. Chiles Research Institute

Research Fellow · 2024–Present

Research focus: Role of ARF6-GTP in mediating tumor-cell intrinsic defense and repair mechanisms that contribute to resistance to T-cell attack.


  • Developing and validating experimental methods and protocols.
  • Studying cytokine-mediated melanoma cell death.
  • Investigating whether ARF6 knockdown sensitizes melanoma cells to immune attack.
  • Maintaining melanoma cell lines and presenting research findings.

Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center

Research Associate · 2023–2024

Research focused on developing patient-specific antigenic T cells for renal cell carcinoma using immunogenic cell death.


  • Developed methods to generate tumor-reactive CD8 T cells.
  • Developed clear cell renal cell carcinoma organoid models.
  • Performed FFPE processing and CA9 staining.
  • Worked with clinical mouse models of ccRCC.

Medical College of Wisconsin

Postdoctoral Fellow · 2019–2023

Research focused on the role of SPHK1 and sphingosine-1-phosphate signaling in the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment of ovarian cancer.


  • Isolation and characterization of extracellular vesicles.
  • In-vitro and in-vivo cancer research.
  • Investigation of tumor–immune interactions.
  • Presentation of research at AACR and scientific meetings.
  • Contribution to grants, research papers and review articles.
