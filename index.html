About

I am a PhD-trained cancer biologist with research experience across immuno-oncology, tumor microenvironment biology, extracellular vesicles, T-cell responses, molecular signaling, biomarker discovery, and translational cancer models.

My work has examined how tumor-cell signaling and the tumor microenvironment influence immune suppression, tumor progression, therapeutic resistance, and response to immunotherapy. I have worked across ovarian cancer, renal cell carcinoma, melanoma, and breast cancer research programs.

My research approach combines in vitro and in vivo experimentation, immune-cell functional assays, molecular biology, flow cytometry, patient-derived models, organoid systems, extracellular-vesicle biology, and proteomics/transcriptomics analysis.

Generating renal carcinoma patient-specific antigenic T cells in vitro using immunogenic cell death.
